Professional Journey

DR PARADKAR is an internationally trained orthopaedic Surgeon & pain management specialist with over 15 years of experience treating more than 25000+ patients across India, Oman, and Canada.

He completed his MBBS in 2005 followed by Diplomate Post graduate studies in orthopaedics in 2011. DR PARADKAR pursued his fellowship in Knee Arthroscopy under renowned joint replacement surgeon Dr. Hemant Wakankar.

Later, he received advanced training in orthopaedic pain management through a prestigious one-year fellowship at TPC, Toronto, Canada.

Having returned to India, DR PARADKAR established two clinics in July 2025 with the primary aim of delivering advanced international patient-centric orthopaedic pain management, focusing entirely on Non-Surgical (without Surgery) solutions.
