Our Services

Trigger Points

A trigger point is a very tender point in the formation of a tight band of muscle that may cause tension headaches, neck pains, back pains, and other musculoskeletal pain.

Selective Nerve Root Blocks

SNRBs are required when conservative treatment does not help to alleviate radiating pain, most often from a herniated disc or spinal stenosis.

Facet Joints Intra-articular Injections

Facet joints are tiny, bony joints that run along your spine, making it flexible and movable.

Plantar Fasciitis

Plantar fasciitis is a recurrent and frequently crippling disorder that produces sharp pain at the heel and on the arch of the foot, particularly in the morning.

Splint Application

Splint application involves using either a stiff or bendable tool to fix an injured joint or bone in place.

Lumbar Epidural Injection

Epidural injection is an excellent, relatively painless remedial method that addresses chronic back and leg pain.

Sacro-illiac Joint Injections

The sacroiliac (SI) joints connect the spine at the lower region with the pelvis and are the common cause of pain in the lower back, hip and buttocks.

Tennis Elbow

Tennis elbow, which is also known as lateral epicondylitis, is pain on the outer side of the elbow.

Carpal Tunnel Syndrome

Carpal Tunnel Syndrome is a condition caused by compression of the median nerve in the wrist, leading to numbness, tingling, and weakness in the hand and fingers.

Trigger Finger

The trigger finger is a condition that arises due to inflammation and constriction of the tendon sheath, which protects the tendon and the way it slides.

Caudal Epidural Injections

Epidural caudal injections are an effective, non-surgical procedure that helps in treating lower-back and leg pain originating from lower lumbo sacral spine.

Intra-articular Injections

Intra-articular injections are medical procedures where medication is directly injected into a joint to relieve pain, reduce inflammation, or improve mobility.

Frozen Shoulder

Frozen shoulder is a condition that occurs in the connective tissue surrounding the shoulder joint, the joint capsule, which is inflamed, thickened, and scarred.

Plaster Application

A plaster application is one of the traditional but necessary techniques to stabilise a broken bone or sprained joint.

Facet Joints Intra-articular Injections

Facet joints are tiny, bony joints that run along your spine, making it flexible and movable.

Visco (durolene/synvisc/
monovisc) PRP Injections

Visco (Durolane, Synvisc, Monovisc) and PRP injections are minimally invasive treatments injected into joints to relieve pain, restore lubrication, and promote healing in conditions like osteoarthritis.

Rotator Cuff Tendinopathy

Rotator cuff tendinopathy is a widespread cause of shoulder pain and stiffness that can severely complicate the performance of the simplest exercises, such as reaching overhead or even sleeping.

Scotch Cast Application

A Scotch Cast application incorporates a specialised type of fibreglass that is breathable, waterproof, and lightweight.
