Condition & Treatment Overview

Chronic lower back pain can affect your daily life, making it difficult to sit, walk, or perform routine activities. Lumbar Epidural Injection is a highly effective treatment that helps reduce inflammation and relieve nerve-related pain in the lower spine. At Dr. Paradkar’s Ortho Cure and Pain Relief Clinic, we offer advanced Lumbar Epidural Injection to provide quick and lasting relief without the need for surgery.

This treatment is especially beneficial for conditions like slipped disc, sciatica, and nerve compression. Under the care of a skilled Non Surgical Orthopedic Doctor , patients receive precise and safe treatment. Our approach to Pain Management ensures targeted relief and faster recovery.

BENEFITS OF LUMBAR EPIDURAL INJECTION

Choosing the right treatment can make a significant difference in managing back pain. Lumbar Epidural Injection helps reduce inflammation, relieve nerve pressure, and improve mobility. It is particularly effective for patients suffering from radiating leg pain and chronic lower back issues.
